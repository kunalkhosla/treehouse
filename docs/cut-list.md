# Bill of materials + cut list (8×8 platform, Phase 1)

> All structural lumber **pressure-treated (PT)**, ground-contact rated for posts.
> Quantities include modest waste. **Verify post lengths on site** after footings cure — heights depend on footing-top elevation and the slope.

## Structure

| Item | Spec | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posts | 6×6 PT, 8 ft | 6 | Buy long, cut to a level line. Uphill 3 short, downhill 3 tall. |
| Beams | 2×8 PT, 8 ft | 4 | Two doubled beams (2 plies each), uphill + downhill edge. |
| Joists | 2×8 PT, 8 ft | 11 | 7 field joists @16" oc + 2 rim + 2 extra for doubling at tree opening. |
| Tree-opening headers | 2×8 PT offcuts | (from above) | Double header top + bottom of the 26"×26" hole. |
| Knee braces | 4×4 PT, 8 ft | 2 | Cut into 45° braces, ~6–8 per platform (focus on tall downhill posts). |
| Decking | 5/4×6 PT, 8 ft | 20 | 64 sq ft + waste. |

## Railing

| Item | Spec | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail posts | 4×4 PT, 8 ft | 4 | Cut to ~48" each (36" above deck + bolted to rim). |
| Top/bottom rails | 2×4 PT, 8 ft | 8 | Perimeter minus entry. |
| Balusters | 2×2 PT, 8 ft | ~12 | Rip/cut to ~36"; **<4" gaps**. OR substitute plywood half-walls. |

## Footings

| Item | Spec | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concrete (fast-set) | 60 lb bags | ~15 | ~2–2.5 bags per 10–12" pier × 6, ~42" deep. |
| Tube forms | 10–12" Sonotube | 6 | Or appropriate footing form. |
| Post bases | Simpson ABU66 (standoff) | 6 | Set in wet concrete; keeps post off ground. |
| Gravel | bag | 2–3 | Drainage at footing bottoms. |

## Hardware

| Item | Spec | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carriage bolts | 1/2" × 8" + washers/nuts | ~20 | Beams to posts; beam plies together. |
| Joist hangers | Simpson LUS28 (for 2×8) | ~24 | Every joist end + tree-opening connections. |
| Structural screws | GRK / Simpson SDWS | 1 box | Knee braces, ledgers, general. |
| Deck screws | coated, 2.5–3" | 5 lb | Decking + railing. |
| Hanger nails | Simpson SD / 10d | 1 box | For all hangers (don't substitute deck screws). |

## Tools (have on hand)

Post-hole digger or auger, level + long straightedge, **laser or water level** (to mark beams level across the slope), circular saw, drill/driver + bits, socket set for carriage bolts, speed square, tape, string line + stakes, safety gear.

## Rough cost

~**$700–1,100** at Phase-1 spec (lumber + concrete + hardware), depending on PT prices.
